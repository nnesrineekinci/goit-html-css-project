About Us

Our photography school is designed for everyone interested in this art, regardless of their experience or skill level.

We offer a wide range of courses that cover all aspects of photography, from technical skills and composition to artistic expression and creativity.

Our Courses

At our photography school, we offer a variety of courses that cover all aspects of this fascinating art.

  1. 01 ↗

    Basics of photography

    During this course, you will be able to familiarize yourself with different types of cameras, learn the basics of composition.

  2. 02 ↗

    Landscape photography

    In this course, you will learn how to choose the right angles, frames, and lighting to capture the beauty of nature.

  3. 03 ↗

    Portrait photography

    During this course, you will learn to choose the right lighting, reproduce the mood and expressions of people.
